1. THE PROBLEM
----------------------------------------------------------------
Small, independent businesses (restaurants, hotels, salons,
clinics, gyms, retail, real estate) run on 5-7 disconnected
tools: a website, a booking system, a POS, a payments
processor, ad accounts, a spreadsheet for customers.

None of it talks to each other. Every integration between two
vendors is a place where numbers stop matching. Setting it all
up is a multi-week implementation project most small operators
can't afford to run and can't afford to get wrong.


2. THE SOLUTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
One platform that replaces all of it: website, bookings,
payments, point of sale, marketing, and customer records --
pre-configured for the customer's specific trade, deployed by
a real specialist, live within 24 hours.

Not a generic SaaS toolkit the customer has to configure
themselves. A working business system on day one.


3. HOW DEPLOYMENT WORKS (0 -> 24 HOURS)
----------------------------------------------------------------
HOUR 0   Intake call (40 min) -- map services, hours, menu or
         inventory, payment methods, how they already work.
HOUR 4   Build & configure -- site, booking rules, tax
         settings, floor plan / room types, staff accounts
         assembled from the industry template.
HOUR 12  Payments & hardware -- card processing verified,
         terminals/printers paired, existing data imported
         and reconciled.
HOUR 24  Live and trained -- real orders, real bookings,
         staff walked through every screen they'll touch.

Guarantee: live in 24 hours, or the first month is free.


4. PRODUCT -- FOUR CORE MODULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
ORDERS      Point of sale, kitchen display, delivery tracking,
            live ticket + daily revenue view.
BOOKINGS    Calendar, occupancy, no-show deposits, buffer
            times, double-booking prevention.
MARKETING   AI-run ad spend across Meta, TikTok, Google --
            automatic budget reallocation every 6 hours against
            booked revenue, creative testing included.
CUSTOMERS   Full CRM -- pipeline, contact history, automated
            follow-up and retention.

Design principle: automation with a paper trail. Anything the
platform decides on the customer's behalf is logged in plain
language and can be reversed by them.

7. WHY NOW / COMPETITIVE ANGLE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Generic SaaS (Shopify, Square, Toast, generic CRMs, etc.):
  - Fast to sign up, slow to actually run -- customer does
    the integration work themselves.
  - Point solutions: pick a tool per function, then stitch
    them together.
  - Support optimized for self-serve, not hands-on setup.

Twentyfour:
  - Deployed BY someone, not configured BY the customer.
  - One system per business, not one tool per function.
  - Speed is the product: 24 hours vs weeks-to-months.

The bet: for a business owner running a 30-cover restaurant
or an 8-room hotel, implementation time is the real cost --
not the software price. Winning on speed + done-for-you setup
is a different axis of competition than winning on features.
