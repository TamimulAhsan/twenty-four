05 — Questions

Answered before you ask.

Anything not covered here, our team will answer in writing within one business day.

Is 24 hours realistic for my business?+

Yes, for the overwhelming majority. The clock starts at the intake call and covers configuration, data import, payment activation and training. Complex multi-location or bespoke integration work is quoted separately and stated before you sign.

What happens to my existing website and data?+

We migrate your domain, content, customer list, bookings and product or menu data. Your existing site stays live until the moment we switch over, so there is no window where customers find nothing.

Do I need to buy new hardware?+

Usually not. The point of sale runs in a browser on hardware you already own. Where terminals or printers are required we supply certified units at cost and configure them before delivery.

Who controls the advertising budget?+

You do. You set a ceiling and the platform allocates within it across channels, reporting every change. Media spend is paid to the platforms directly and is not part of your subscription.

Am I locked into a contract?+

Monthly plans are cancellable with thirty days' notice. Your domain remains yours and your data can be exported in full at any time, in open formats, without contacting support.
